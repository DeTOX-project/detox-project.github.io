DeTOX

Determination of Tropical Oxidising Capacity through model calibration

A new collaboration between atmospheric scientists and experts in machine learning. In addition to improved insight into oxidation processes, a key outcome will be development of new statistical methodologies that will benefit the wider atmospheric chemistry and climate communities.

Funded by UKRI / NERC
Project duration 01/04/2026 – 30/09/2029
Led by Lancaster University
Partners 5 institutions

About DeTOX

DeTOX brings together atmospheric scientists and experts in machine learning to improve our understanding of tropical atmospheric oxidation and develop new approaches for constraining atmospheric chemistry models.

Our Research

Tropical oxidation

Investigate the processes that govern tropical oxidising capacity and its variability.

Bring a clear characterization of the oxidising capacity across tropical environments

Model calibration

Use observations and statistical methods to constrain atmospheric chemistry models.

Get a robust quantification of the processes controlling OH and oxidation constrained by atmospheric measurements.

Future projection

Develop new statistical and machine-learning approaches for atmospheric chemistry and climate research.

Conduct an innovative process-based assessment of future changes in oxidation, including the implications for methane and other key pollutants.
